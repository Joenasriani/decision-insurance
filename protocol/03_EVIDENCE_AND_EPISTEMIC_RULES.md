# Evidence and Epistemic Rules

## Definitions

### Claim
A proposition that can be evaluated.

### Evidence
A source-grounded observation or fact relevant to a claim.

### Inference
A conclusion derived from evidence.

### Assumption
A proposition required by reasoning but not adequately established.

### Contradiction
Evidence or reasoning that materially conflicts with another claim.

### Unknown
Information required for evaluation but not established.

## Support Rule

A claim may be `SUPPORTED` only if:

- linked evidence directly or adequately supports it
- evidence is relevant
- provenance exists
- temporal validity is acceptable
- no unresolved critical contradiction defeats it
- inference distance is acceptable

## Partial Support Rule

Use `PARTIAL` when:

- only part of the claim is supported
- evidence is indirect
- evidence is temporally limited
- evidence supports a weaker formulation
- a material dimension remains unresolved

## Unsupported Rule

Use `UNSUPPORTED` when:

- the claim lacks adequate evidence
- available evidence does not establish it
- reasoning extends beyond source facts
- evidence is irrelevant to the proposition

## Assumption Rule

Use `ASSUMPTION` when a proposition is needed for the reasoning but is not established.

## Contradiction Rule

Use `CONTRADICTED` when credible evidence materially conflicts with the claim.

## Unknown Rule

Use `UNKNOWN` when information is insufficient, absent, unreadable, ambiguous, or inconclusive.

## Independence Rule

Multiple items count as independent corroboration only when they derive from meaningfully independent source lineages.

## Evidence Strength

Evidence strength is separate from claim status:

- HIGH
- MEDIUM
- LOW
- UNASSESSED

## No Self-Support

A model-generated statement cannot serve as evidence for itself or another model-generated statement without source grounding.

## No Authority Substitution

Prestige does not replace logical relevance.

## No Forced Closure

The system MUST preserve PARTIAL, ASSUMPTION, CONTRADICTED, and UNKNOWN states where appropriate.
